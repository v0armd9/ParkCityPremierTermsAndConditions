Booking

Reservations are booked through the Park City Premier app. The app applies our minimum notice and minimum trip length when you choose a pickup time and addresses. A reservation stays pending until a Local Service Provider accepts it; when one does, the app shows the reservation as confirmed. If no Local Service Provider accepts it, the Unfulfilled Reservations section below applies.

A reservation cannot be edited in the app after it is booked. To change a reservation, cancel it and book again under the Cancelation, Change, and No-Show Policy below, or call Park City Premier at +1 (801) 707-8570.

Vehicle Substitution

Park City Premier and the Local Service Providers reserve the right to substitute a comparable or upgraded vehicle for the vehicle class originally booked where the scheduled vehicle is unavailable, provided the substitute vehicle meets or exceeds the passenger, luggage, car seat, wheelchair, and pet accommodation requirements of the original reservation. A substitution does not change the price of the reservation. Park City Premier does not guarantee the provision of a specific vehicle, make, or model, or a specific chauffeur, for any reservation.

Booking Accuracy — Passengers, Luggage, Car Seats, Wheelchairs, and Pets

At the time of booking, the Guest is responsible for accurately declaring, through the Park City Premier app: (a) the number of passengers traveling; (b) any large or oversized luggage, including golf clubs, skis, snowboards, or similar items; (c) any car seat requirements, including rear-facing, forward-facing, or booster seats; (d) whether a passenger needs space for a manual wheelchair; and (e) whether a service or companion animal will be traveling with the Guest. Park City Premier and the Local Service Provider rely on this information to assign a vehicle of adequate size and configuration for the reservation. Vehicles do not have wheelchair ramps or lifts.

Where a Guest requests a car seat through the app, the Local Service Provider will provide a car seat as available. The Guest remains responsible for confirming that the car seat provided is suitable for the child's age, height, and weight in accordance with applicable law, and for properly securing the child, unless the chauffeur agrees to assist. Park City Premier does not guarantee the availability of a specific car seat type for any reservation.

If the Guest's actual passenger count, luggage, car seat needs, wheelchair, or accompanying animal at pickup exceeds or differs from what was declared at booking, the Local Service Provider may, at its discretion: (i) accommodate the difference where the assigned vehicle reasonably and safely permits; or (ii) decline to transport the excess passengers, luggage, or animal where the assigned vehicle cannot safely or lawfully accommodate them. Park City Premier does not add charges to a reservation after it is booked; where the Guest's party cannot travel as booked, the Guest may book an additional ride. Park City Premier is not liable for delay, additional charges, or inability to complete a reservation resulting from inaccurate information provided by the Guest at booking.

A reservation terminated under this section due to Guest conduct or undeclared passengers, luggage, car seat needs, or animals will be treated as a no-show or late cancelation, as applicable, under the Cancelation, Change, and No-Show Policy below.

Cancelation, Change, and No-Show Policy

You may cancel a reservation in the app at no charge until 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time. A late cancelation charge applies when a reservation is canceled within 2 hours of the scheduled pickup time. This charge equals the full fare for the reservation (the base rate and mileage), not including any tip. The app shows the charge before you confirm a cancelation. This policy applies to every vehicle class.

To change a reservation, cancel it and book again, or call Park City Premier's dispatch/support number, +1 (801) 707-8570.

If you cannot locate your assigned chauffeur at the pickup location, contact the chauffeur through the app, or call Park City Premier, before leaving the pickup location. If you have not met your chauffeur by the end of the applicable wait-time grace period below, the reservation may be canceled as a no-show and charged the late cancelation charge. We will try to work with you when unforeseen circumstances arise, but the grace period is the only wait we guarantee.

Wait Time — Airport Pickups

For commercial flight pickups, your chauffeur will wait up to twenty (20) minutes after the scheduled pickup time at no charge, provided you proceed directly to the designated meeting point. After that, the no-show provisions of the Cancelation, Change, and No-Show Policy apply.

Flight Delays

Flight delays are unforeseen and outside the control of both the Guest and Park City Premier. Park City Premier and its Local Service Providers will make reasonable efforts to accommodate flight delays and adjust pickup timing accordingly at no additional charge. However, where a Guest's flight is delayed more than one (1) hour beyond its originally scheduled arrival time, Park City Premier and its Local Service Providers may, in rare cases, be unable to provide the reserved Services for that booking. Where Park City Premier and its Local Service Providers are unable to provide Services due to a flight delay, the Unfulfilled Reservations section above applies and the Guest will not be charged.

Wait Time — Point-to-Point Pickups

Your chauffeur will wait up to fifteen (15) minutes after the scheduled pickup time at no charge. After that, the no-show provisions of the Cancelation, Change, and No-Show Policy apply.

Additional Stops

Reservations are booked from one pickup to one drop-off. A single additional stop of up to fifteen (15) minutes may be included at no extra charge. A longer stop, or more than one stop, requires a separate reservation.

Pricing

Your price is a base rate plus a per-mile rate for the vehicle class you choose, plus any tip you add. If taxes apply, they appear in the price breakdown. Before you pay, the app shows each part and the total. You pay that total when the ride is complete, and we never add to it. Rates can change, but a change never affects a reservation you have already booked.
